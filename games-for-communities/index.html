Memory Games for Senior Communities

Warm, hands-on matching games your residents can hold, see, and enjoy.

We are not a corporate company — we are a family. I created these games from scratch, and I first saw what they could do while playing with my own mother. That is the whole reason I bring them to senior communities.

They are simple tile-matching games with real, easy-to-hold pieces. Matching is a calm, familiar way for seniors to stay mentally active, and it works beautifully in a group or one-on-one, including for residents living with memory challenges.

  • Gentle, screen-free cognitive activity
  • Large, easy-to-see and easy-to-hold tiles
  • Great for group activity time or quiet one-on-one moments
  • Several themes, so there is something for everyone
