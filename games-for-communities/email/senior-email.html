My name is Ivan Duran, and I own Duty Free Designs, a small business here in Houston. I know your inbox stays full, so I will keep this short.

Here is how I ended up bringing my games to senior communities. I was filming while playing one with my mother and my mother-in-law, and I watched them light up — laughing, remembering, and genuinely working their memory. I thought, if it does that for the women in my family, it could do it for a whole room of residents.

We are a family, and we make several of these games — Nostalgic Memories, Cats and Dogs, Baby Monsters, Pandas, and more. I have designed almost all of them myself, from scratch. They are warm, familiar images on real, easy-to-hold tiles — calm, screen-free, and they bring families in when they visit.
