Keep your brain sharp.

The matching-tile game that's fun for every age — from the grandkids to grandma. Here's how it works, and why it's good for you.

1How to play

  • Lay the tiles face down in a grid.
  • Flip two at a time — if they match, keep the pair and go again.
  • If they don't match, flip them back and remember where they were.
  • Most matched pairs wins. Play solo, in pairs, or as a whole family.

2Why it keeps you sharp

Matching games are a simple, proven brain workout — no screen required.

  • Memory: you're constantly recalling where tiles are.
  • Focus: short, satisfying rounds that hold attention.
  • Connection: a reason to sit together and play — great for seniors and kids alike.
